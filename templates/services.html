Testing & Quality Verification

At Chipstock, quality isn't just a checkbox — it's a non-negotiable standard. Every component we handle goes through a rigorous multi-step verification process to ensure authenticity, functionality, and compliance. We adhere to AS6081 and IDEA-STD-1010 guidelines, partnering with certified third-party labs to perform detailed inspections, including visual analysis, dimensional checks, X-ray and XRF testing, blacktop and remarking detection, decapsulation, and functional electrical testing when required. Our team documents every step, providing full traceability and a Certificate of Conformance with each shipment. These procedures help protect your production lines from counterfeit or substandard components, reduce risk, and maintain compliance with demanding industry standards. Whether you're sourcing active parts or critical legacy components, you can trust Chipstock to deliver quality that's verified — not assumed.
