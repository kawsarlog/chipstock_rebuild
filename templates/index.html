02/ Our Services

All Services
01
Shortage Sourcing
When supply chains break down, we step in. Chipstock specializes in sourcing critical components during market shortages, helping you avoid production delays and costly downtime. With a global network and real-time inventory access, we deliver the parts you need — fast, verified, and hassle-free.
02
Testing & Quality Verification
Every component undergoes thorough inspection and compliance screening. Our testing process follows AS6081 standards and includes blacktop inspection, XRF analysis, decapsulation, and electrical testing — with COC documentation included in every shipment.
03
Excess Inventory Management
Unlock value from your excess inventory — effortlessly. We create custom solutions through direct buyouts, demand matching, or consignment to help you recover maximum return while you stay focused on your core business.
04
Obsolete / End-of-Life Sourcing
When critical components become obsolete or unavailable through standard channels, Chipstock helps you keep production moving. We specialize in sourcing end-of-life parts through our trusted global network, delivering verified, traceable components for legacy systems and long-lifecycle programs. Whether you’re avoiding redesigns or supporting repairs, we provide reliable solutions that extend the life of your products — without compromising quality or compliance.
